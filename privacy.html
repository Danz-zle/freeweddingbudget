Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Wedding Budget Planner handles calculator data, browser storage, website security, analytics, advertising, cookies, and your privacy choices.

No account requiredThe calculator does not ask you to register or create a user profile.
Budget entries stay localWedding budget amounts are designed to remain in the browser on the device you use.
Third-party services are disclosedCloudflare and Google services may process technical, analytics, or advertising information.

1. Who operates this website

Wedding Budget Planner is an independent website created and maintained by Dan. Liu. Questions about this policy, corrections, or privacy concerns can be sent to liubigcow@yahoo.com.

2. Calculator data and local browser storage

The calculator is designed to process wedding budget entries in your web browser. The site does not require an account and does not intentionally transmit the amounts you enter, category allocations, guest counts, or expense descriptions to the site owner's own database.

The calculator may use browser storage, such as local storage, to keep entries available on the same browser and device. This information can remain after the page is closed. It may disappear when you clear site data, use private browsing, reset the browser, change devices, or use a different browser profile.

Your control: you can remove locally saved calculator data by using the calculator's reset controls where available or by clearing browser storage and site data for freeweddingbudget.com.

4. Cloudflare and Cloudflare Zaraz

The website uses Cloudflare for services such as content delivery, performance, network security, and protection against malicious traffic. Cloudflare may process technical request information as part of providing those services.

Google Analytics may be loaded through Cloudflare Zaraz. Zaraz is a tool-management service that can load analytics or other third-party services from Cloudflare's network. The analytics configuration is intended to measure website usage and is not intended to collect the wedding budget values entered into the calculator.

5. Google Analytics

Google Analytics may be used to create aggregated reports about page views, traffic sources, device types, country-level or approximate location information, and visitor engagement. These reports help identify which calculator functions and planning guides are useful or need improvement.

Google may process analytics information under its own terms and privacy policy. Browser settings, consent controls where presented, and privacy tools provided by Google may affect analytics storage or measurement.

6. Google AdSense, cookies, and similar technologies

This website may display advertisements through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies in your browser or use web beacons, IP addresses, and other identifiers as a result of ad serving on this website.

Google and its partners may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website or other websites, subject to the visitor's region, consent choices, browser settings, and Google's advertising settings. Non-personalized ads can still use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated reporting, fraud prevention, and ad delivery.

7. Consent and privacy choices

Depending on your location and the website's configured privacy settings, you may be shown a consent or privacy-choice message before certain advertising or analytics technologies are used. Your choices may influence whether ads are personalized and whether analytics or advertising storage is permitted.

  • Manage Google ad personalization through Google Ads Settings.
  • Use your browser controls to block or delete cookies and site storage.
  • Use any consent or privacy-options message displayed on the site to review available choices.
  • Clearing cookies or browser storage may reset previously saved privacy choices.

8. Third-party links and services

Planning articles may link to official agencies, consumer guidance, research sources, or other websites. A link does not mean Wedding Budget Planner controls that website or its privacy practices. Review the privacy notice of the destination site before providing personal information.

9. Children's privacy

Wedding Budget Planner is intended for general wedding-budget planning and is not directed to children under 13. The site does not knowingly ask children to create accounts or submit personal information through the calculator.

10. Retention and deletion

Calculator information saved in browser storage remains under the control of that browser until it is overwritten, reset, or cleared. Email messages may be retained as needed to answer questions or maintain a record of corrections. Retention by Cloudflare, Google, advertisers, or linked websites is governed by those providers' own policies and settings.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the calculator, analytics setup, advertising services, consent tools, or legal requirements change. The visible update date at the top of the page will be changed when a material revision is published.

Important limitation: this policy describes the site's current general practices but is not legal advice and does not create rights beyond those provided by applicable law and the terms of the relevant third-party services.
