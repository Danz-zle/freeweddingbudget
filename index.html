Wedding Budget Planning Tips

A useful wedding budget calculator helps couples track every expense from venue deposits to floral arrangements. Start by setting your total budget, then divide funds into major categories such as catering, photography, decor, attire, and entertainment.

Many couples underestimate hidden costs such as service charges, gratuities, alterations, delivery, vendor meals, and transportation. Tracking payments in one place can reduce stress and make trade-offs easier before final invoices arrive.
