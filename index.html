Calculation method

How the planner handles your numbers

Vendor true cost
Package price + required fees + travel or delivery + rentals + likely overtime + tax on that subtotal.
Committed budget
Only selected quotes count as commitments. Other saved quotes remain available for comparison.
Payments
Deposits and balances show cash flow. They do not add a second copy of the vendor contract to the budget.
Guest projection
The planner keeps non-catering costs, replaces tracked catering with guest count × catering per guest, then compares the result with the total budget.

Planning estimates are not vendor quotes. Check the taxable subtotal, payment terms, service scope, and local rules before signing.
