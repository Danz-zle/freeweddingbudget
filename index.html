Plan the full cost of your wedding

Wedding Budget Planner helps couples set category limits, compare the full cost of vendor quotes, track payments, test different guest counts and download planning reports. It is free, requires no account and saves your plan only in your browser, not on a server.

Start with your total budget, then record what each vendor will actually cost after required fees, delivery, rentals, likely overtime and tax. Deposits and later payments remain connected to the original commitment, so the same cost is not counted twice.

Start with a budget you can control

The budget control center separates different types of wedding costs instead of placing everything into one total.

You can set a planned limit for each category and compare it with selected vendor commitments and other expenses. This helps you see how much remains available in categories such as venue, catering, photography, decor, entertainment, attire and transportation.

The overview brings the main figures together:

  • Total wedding budget
  • Selected vendor commitments
  • Completed payments
  • Other expenses
  • Uncommitted budget
  • Next payment due

The currency selector supports USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, NZD, SGD, MYR, TWD and HKD. Changing the currency changes the displayed symbol only. It does not convert your amounts or apply an exchange rate.

Compare the true cost, not only the package price

A lower advertised package does not always mean a lower final cost.

Wedding Budget Planner uses this method:

Package price + required fees + travel or delivery + rentals + likely overtime + tax on that subtotal

In a fictional photography example, Silverline starts with a package price that is $300 lower, but Willow ends $108 lower after the same extras and an 8% tax assumption are included. See the full fictional comparison and calculation.

You can save several quotes while comparing them. When a vendor is selected as a commitment, its true cost becomes part of the budget. A deposit or installment is recorded as a payment against that commitment, not as another copy of the contract cost.

Keep payment dates connected to the plan

A signed contract and a paid invoice are not the same thing. The payment tracker keeps the committed vendor cost separate from the amounts already paid.

You can record deposits, installments and remaining balances with their due dates. The tracker shows each payment's due date and status, plus the next payment due.

Marking a payment as paid reduces the amount still payable, but it does not change the vendor's contract cost. This makes it possible to see both the complete cost of the agreement and the amount that still needs to be paid.

Test what happens when the guest count changes

The guest-count scenario planner lets you compare different attendance assumptions before changing your main plan.

It keeps your non-catering costs fixed and replaces the catering cost with your guest count multiplied by your estimated catering cost per guest, then compares the total with your budget.

A guest-count scenario is a planning comparison. It does not replace a vendor quote or automatically convert an estimate into a commitment.

Your plan stays in your browser

Wedding Budget Planner does not require registration. Your wedding plan is saved only in the browser you are using, not on a server.

This also means that the plan does not automatically appear on another device or browser. Clearing the browser's saved data may remove it.

The backup feature lets you download a copy of the complete Wedding Budget Planner record. You can keep that file and restore it later in a compatible browser session. The reset option removes the Wedding Budget Planner data saved in the current browser.

Use the backup function before clearing browser data or moving your plan to another browser.

Download a planning report

When you are ready to review or share the figures, the Reports section can export the plan as:

  • Excel workbook
  • Print-ready PDF
  • CSV files (vendors, payments, expenses)

Wedding Budget Planner also provides a backup and restore file for moving the complete saved planning record. A report is for reviewing the figures; the backup file is for restoring the planner data later.
