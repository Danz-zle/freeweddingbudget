The Ultimate Wedding Planning Timeline Checklist

WB
Written by Wedding Budget Planner
Practical budgeting guide • Updated June 2026

A timeline is useful because wedding planning has many deadlines that affect your budget. Booking too late can reduce choice, but booking too quickly can lead to deposits before you understand the full cost.

Use this timeline as a flexible checklist. Adjust it for your event size, location, travel needs, and whether you are planning a simple ceremony or a full reception.

12 to 9 months before: set the foundation

  • Discuss your total budget and decide who is contributing.
  • Draft the first guest list because guest count affects almost every major cost.
  • Research venues and ask for full estimates, not just rental fees.
  • Book the venue only after you understand catering, service charges, rental rules, and payment deadlines.

8 to 6 months before: book core vendors

  • Compare photography, catering, music, flowers, and planning support.
  • Record deposit amounts and final balance due dates in your budget tracker.
  • Start attire decisions early enough to allow shipping and alterations.
  • Build a rough decor plan before buying small items.

5 to 3 months before: refine details

This is when small decisions begin to add up. Invitations, signage, favors, cake, beauty trials, rentals, transportation, and hotel blocks can all create extra costs. Review your remaining budget before approving upgrades.

2 months before: confirm guest count pressure

Send invitations with a clear RSVP deadline. The final guest count affects food, drinks, table layouts, rentals, printed items, and sometimes staffing. Late RSVP handling can create stress, so build a follow-up plan.

Final month: protect the budget

Review all remaining balances, payment methods, tip envelopes, vendor meals, arrival times, setup rules, and cleanup expectations. Many last-minute costs happen because responsibilities are unclear.

Practical note:

Set your RSVP deadline about five weeks before the wedding if your caterer needs final numbers several weeks in advance.
