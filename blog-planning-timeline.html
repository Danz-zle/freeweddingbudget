The Ultimate Wedding Planning Timeline Checklist

By Project Operations Desk • Published May 2026 • 9 min read

Managing vendor deadlines and scheduling milestones keeps your planning organized. Spacing out tasks across structured calendar blocks reduces stress and avoids premium, last-minute rush fees.

Advertisement

Phase 1: Initial Framework (12 to 9 Months Out)

Focus on foundational booking requirements during your initial planning phase:

  • Determine total spending boundaries using tracking tools before negotiating vendor contracts.
  • Research and lock in target dates with your primary ceremony and reception venues.
  • Outline your core guest list draft to set appropriate catering and space parameters.
PRO TIP: Vendor Scheduling Priorities High-demand photo teams and bands book out early. Secure your essential media and entertainment providers right after confirming your venue dates.

Phase 2: Core Details (6 to 3 Months Out)

Move into regular vendor coordination and logistics during mid-tier planning blocks:

  • Finalize invitation designs and prepare your distribution timeline.
  • Coordinate menu choices and banquet structures with your catering team.
  • Schedule fitting sessions to allow plenty of time for custom garment alterations.
