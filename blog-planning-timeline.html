The Ultimate Wedding Planning Timeline Checklist

EP
Sarah Mitchell Wedding Planning Expert

A well-planned timeline ensures you make progress consistently without feeling overwhelmed. Spacing out tasks chronologically helps you stay ahead of important deadlines, avoid premium booking fees, and keep your overall stress to a minimum.

Advertisement

Phase 1: Initial Framework (12 to 9 Months Out)

Focus on foundational booking requirements during your initial planning phase. Do not skip these steps, as popular venues and vendors book out up to a year in advance:

  • Determine total spending boundaries using tracking tools before negotiating vendor contracts.
  • Research and lock in target dates with your primary ceremony and reception venues.
  • Outline your core guest list draft to set appropriate catering and space parameters.
  • Research and book your wedding coordinator, photographer, and videographer.

Phase 2: Sourcing and Selection (8 to 6 Months Out)

Once your date and venue are officially locked in, begin building your vendor team and selecting design components:

  • Shop for and order your wedding gown and bridal party attire (leaving ample time for manufacturing and shipping).
  • Book secondary key vendors, including your florist, caterer, DJ/band, and block hotel rooms for out-of-town guests.
  • Launch your wedding website with venue details, travel information, and registry links.
  • Schedule engagement photos with your booked photographer.

Phase 3: Coordination and Details (5 to 3 Months Out)

This is where your vision begins to align with operational logistics. Fine-tune your contracts and timeline specifics:

  • Finalize your paper suite and order your invitations, RSVP cards, and menus.
  • Meet with your florist or decor team to finalize floral designs and table mockups.
  • Buy your wedding bands and schedule your initial wedding dress and suit fittings.
  • Book any necessary rental elements, such as custom tableware, special lighting, or a getaway car.

Phase 4: The Home Stretch (2 Months to 1 Week Out)

In the final weeks leading up to the celebration, transition from procurement to coordination:

  • Send formal wedding invitations to your finalized guest list (aiming for RSVP deadlines 4 to 5 weeks before the wedding).
  • Finalize the wedding day timeline with your coordinator and distribute it to all vendors.
  • Create your seating chart using final RSVP data, and print your seating displays and place cards.
  • Submit final headcount metrics to your caterer and complete your final vendor payments.
