About This Website

Free Wedding Budget Calculator is a simple planning tool designed to help couples estimate, organize, and track wedding-related expenses.

The purpose of this website is to provide an easy way to:

  • Allocate a total wedding budget across different expense categories
  • Track actual spending as planning progresses
  • Understand remaining budget and cost per guest
  • Export budget summaries to Excel or PDF for personal use

This tool does not require account creation or personal registration. All calculations are performed directly in your browser for convenience and privacy.

This website is intended for personal planning purposes only and does not provide financial or legal advice.
