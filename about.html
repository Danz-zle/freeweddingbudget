About Wedding Budget Planner

Wedding Budget Planner is a small personal project built to help couples organize wedding costs without signing up, installing software, or starting from a complicated spreadsheet. The goal is simple: give visitors a practical calculator and clear planning guides that make wedding budgeting easier to understand.

Who Runs This Site

This site is maintained by L. Liu as a low-budget independent publishing project. I built the calculator with a practical engineering mindset: keep the tool lightweight, make the numbers visible, and avoid collecting unnecessary personal information from visitors.

I am not a wedding agency, financial adviser, or large media company. The articles are written as general planning resources and are updated when I improve the tool or find clearer ways to explain wedding budgeting decisions.

Editorial Approach

The guides on this site focus on practical budgeting habits: setting a realistic ceiling, separating must-have expenses from flexible extras, tracking deposits, and leaving room for hidden costs. I try to avoid exaggerated claims and vendor-style sales language.

  • Practical examples: Articles use sample budgets, category ranges, and checklists so readers can apply the advice to their own plans.
  • No fake authority: This site does not claim to be a certified planning firm or a professional financial service.
  • No paid vendor rankings: The site does not sell wedding vendor placements or publish paid vendor recommendations.

Disclaimer

The calculator, checklists, and articles are provided for general informational and educational purposes only. They are not personal financial or professional wedding-planning advice. Always review your own budget carefully before signing vendor contracts.
