The most current large US figure available in July 2026 is $34,200. The Knot's 2026 Real Weddings Study says this was the overall average among 10,474 US couples who married during 2025. That wording matters: it is data published in 2026 about completed 2025 weddings, not a guaranteed price for a wedding booked today.

Zola's 2025 First Look Report surveyed more than 6,000 couples planning 2025 weddings, but its currently published report focuses on planning behavior and trends rather than providing a directly comparable completed-wedding average. It should not be used as support for a second national cost figure here.

The average is context, not a target

  1. Use it for context. It shows the scale of spending reported by a survey sample.
  2. Do not copy it blindly. An arithmetic average can be pulled upward by higher-spend events.
  3. Match the event scope. Guest count, location, venue, season, vendors, and included events change the comparison.
  4. Build from local evidence. Complete written quotes and required fees are more useful than one national number.

The study labels are easy to misread. "2026 average wedding cost" can refer to a report released in 2026, even when its respondents married in 2025. When comparing articles, look for the wedding year, sample size, geography, whether the amount is projected or completed spending, and which expenses are included.

Guest count changes the comparison, but not every cost

The Knot's current study reports an average guest count of 117 and an average cost per guest of $292. It also reports different overall averages by attendance band. These are survey findings, not price quotes, but they demonstrate why a single national total is incomplete.

Guest-count bandThe Knot reported averageHow to interpret it
1 to 50 guests$17,100Smaller attendance can reduce guest-linked costs, but venue minimums and fixed vendors may remain.
51 to 100 guests$27,200More food, drinks, rentals, stationery, tables, and staffing usually enter the budget.
More than 100 guests$43,300Capacity thresholds may require a larger venue, more staff, security, transport, and equipment.

Do not multiply $292 by your guest count and assume the result is a quote. Some costs are mostly fixed, some scale per guest, and others jump only after a venue or staffing threshold. Use the 50 versus 100 guests guide to model those differences.

Location can move the average more than one upgrade

The same study reports averages of $32,600 for hometown weddings, $41,700 for domestic destination weddings, and $43,700 for international destination weddings. It also shows wide differences between states and cities. These figures do not make every destination wedding more expensive. They do show why travel, accommodation, labor, delivery, sourcing, and the local venue market belong in the comparison.

The couple can now change guest count, venue, meal style, priorities, or date and see which decisions move the total.

Replace the national number with your own evidence

  1. Enter the maximum amount you can safely use in Budget; do not copy the survey average.
  2. Use Guests to save lower and higher attendance scenarios with a quote-based catering assumption.
  3. Enter complete local quotes in Vendors, separating package price, required fees, travel, rentals, overtime, and tax.
  4. Select only the quote you intend to treat as committed, then schedule its deposits and balances in Payments.
  5. Keep part of the ceiling uncommitted while important quotes or guest counts remain uncertain.

FAQ

Is $34,200 what a wedding should cost in 2026?

No. It is The Knot's survey average for US couples married in 2025. Your safe budget can be far below or above it depending on event type, location, guest count, priorities, and available funds.

Why can two wedding surveys produce different results?

They may cover different wedding years, locations, expense definitions, respondent groups, and projected versus completed spending. Compare the source and methodology before treating two figures as equivalent.

Should I use average vendor prices in the calculator?

Only as temporary placeholders. Replace them with quotes for the same date, city, hours, guest count, staffing, deliverables, and required fees before making a booking decision.

What matters more than the national average?

Your safe ceiling, local quotes, guest count, venue rules, full contract totals, payment schedule, and reserve matter more because they determine whether the plan is actually affordable.
