DIY Wedding Decor That Looks Professional

DL
Written by Dan. Liu / Wedding Budget Planner
Practical budgeting guide • Updated June 2026

DIY decor can save money when the projects are simple, repeatable, and finished before the wedding day. The goal is to make the room feel intentional without adding transport problems or last-minute setup stress.

Examples use USD for simplicity. You can adapt the calculator to your local currency and vendor quotes. Flower prices, candle rules, venue setup time, and rental costs vary by location.

Choose projects that scale

Good DIY options include table numbers, printed signs, bud vases, candle groupings, welcome table details, and simple photo displays. Riskier projects include heavy backdrops, ceiling pieces, and anything that requires difficult setup on the wedding morning.

Sample decor scenario

For a 10 table reception, a couple might budget $400 for candles, $300 for small vases, $250 for greenery or simple stems, $150 for printed signage, and $200 for backup supplies. That $1,300 example can feel polished if the colors and materials repeat.

Checklist

  • Ask whether open flame is allowed.
  • Confirm room access time.
  • Test one table before buying supplies for every table.
  • Use containers that are easy to transport.
  • Assign setup and cleanup to someone other than the couple.

Common mistakes

The biggest mistake is choosing projects that look nice online but are hard to repeat. Another mistake is buying supplies without checking table size.
