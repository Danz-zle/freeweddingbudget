DIY Wedding Decor That Looks Professional (Without Breaking Your Budget)

SM
Sarah Mitchell Wedding Planning Expert

The floral and decor budget for a typical 150-guest wedding runs $4,000 to $7,000 when handled by professionals. That includes ceremony arrangements, reception centerpieces, bridal bouquet, bridesmaid flowers, boutonnieres, and accent pieces. For couples with more creativity than cash, a strategic DIY approach can cut this to $1,500-$2,500 while still looking stunning in person and in photos.

DIY Floral Centerpieces

Professional centerpieces average $75-$150 each. DIY versions using grocery store flowers and greenery cost $15-$30 each. The secret is choosing flowers that look expensive but are not: carnations (when clustered tightly), baby's breath, eucalyptus, and seasonal blooms from wholesale flower markets.

  • Buy flowers 2 days before the wedding, not earlier. Most grocery store flowers last 5-7 days.
  • Use bulk-bought clear glass cylinder vases and floating candles to add high-end ambient light at low costs.
