DIY Wedding Decor That Looks Professional (Without Breaking Your Budget)

SM
Written by Sarah Mitchell
Wedding Planning Expert • Updated April 2026

The floral and decor budget for a typical 150-guest wedding runs $4,000 to $7,000 when handled entirely by professionals. That includes ceremony arrangements, reception centerpieces, bouquets, and backdrops. For creative couples, a strategic DIY approach can cut this to under $1,500 while still looking completely stunning.

The secret to successful DIY is simplicity, consistency, and focusing your efforts on items that can be pre-assembled. In this guide, we'll walk you through how to source bulk elements, curate cohesive centerpieces, and design signage that feels expensive.

Strategy 1: High-Density Tabletop Greenery

Standard flower arrangements are expensive because focal flowers like garden roses, ranunculus, and peonies are highly delicate and cost a premium per stem. You can bypass this by designing tabletop centerpieces that focus heavily on structural, hardy greenery.

  • Choose the Right Stems: Buy silver dollar eucalyptus, Italian ruscus, or salal in bulk from wholesale distributors (like Flower Moxie or a local grocery florist). These greens are incredibly hardy and look lush even if arranged days in advance.
  • The Garland Illusion: Instead of paying for hand-woven floral garlands, buy loose greenery branches and layer them directly down the center of your tables. Tuck in clear glass votives and floating candles to add warmth.

Strategy 2: The Magic of Cozy Candlelight

Candlelight masks minor design flaws, softens shadows, and adds instant warmth to any room. It is the single cheapest way to make a space feel incredibly romantic and high-end.

Buy clear glass cylinder sleeves in bulk online, along with white pillar candles in varying heights (typically 3-inch, 4-inch, and 6-inch cylinders). Cluster three cylinders in the center of each table, surrounded by loose eucalyptus leaves. This simple design costs roughly $15 to $20 per table, compared to professional arrangements that start at $120.

PRO DECOR INSIGHT

Always check your venue’s candle policies. Many historical properties or ballroom spaces prohibit open flames. If this is the case, buy high-quality, real-wax LED candles. Avoid cheap plastic LED candles, as their yellow, flickering light can look unnatural in photos.

Strategy 3: What You Should Never DIY

The biggest pitfall for DIY couples is overestimating their time and energy on the actual morning of the wedding. Never attempt to DIY complicated ceiling drapery, complex overhead lighting, or large-scale floral arches. These projects require special equipment, tall ladders, and hours of manual labor that will exhaust you or your bridal party right before you walk down the aisle. Focus your efforts entirely on portable, tabletop items.
