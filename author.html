I am Dan Liu, the independent site builder behind Wedding Budget Planner. I am not a wedding professional, financial adviser, lawyer, venue operator or vendor marketplace.

I have been married for over ten years. I built the planner as a project to make the full cost of vendor quotes easier to compare.

Wedding Budget Planner is free, needs no account and stores planning data only in the visitor's browser.

How the guides are written

The guides use fictional examples, and each fictional example is labeled as fictional. When a guide includes survey figures, it links to the organization that published them.

Several guides now compare real published fee pages and hotel price lists. Each links to its source, gives the date I read it, and says where the source has limits.

The site does not sell vendor rankings or placement. Its examples do not promote any venue, caterer, photographer or retailer.

If I find an error, I fix it. When I make a real change to a page, I update its displayed date.
